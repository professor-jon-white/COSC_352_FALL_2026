Question 1 Financial Liability Metric

Stakeholder Explanation: Aggregates municipal financial settlement liabilities alongside total incident counts per police district to highlight fiscal exposure across municipal administrative zones.

Question 2 Operational Focus

Units & Shifts Hotspots

Stakeholder Explanation: Identifies operational units and working shifts present in cross-matched settlement records (association, not correlation) to assist leadership in shift-level oversight.

Question 3 Temporal Velocity Analysis

Reporting & Discipline Timeline

Stakeholder Explanation: Compares annual counts of reported misconduct cases against formal disciplinary actions over time to measure process throughput and resolution lag.

Question 4 Recurrence Metric

Recurring Officer Identifiers

Stakeholder Explanation: Highlights repeat officer identifiers appearing in multiple records, tracking individual cost impact relative to the departmental mean officer payout.

Question 5 Composite Risk Model

District Resource Allocation Risk Index

Stakeholder Explanation: Calculates a composite risk score (0–100 scale) combining financial liability weightings (60%) and incident density (40%) to aid strategic resource allocation.

Data Limitations & Policy Disclaimer

Demonstration inputs are synthetic and normalized for analytical benchmarking. Statistics are descriptive and do not establish direct causal relationships.

A matched row uses stable officer_id records supplied in both datasets; district, unit, and calendar-year comparisons depend strictly on the supplied normalized fields.
