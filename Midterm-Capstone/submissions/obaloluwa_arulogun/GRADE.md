### Overview
A multi-package Go program (cmd, internal/seed, ingest, engine, model, render) that loads two CSVs through channel-based worker pools with sync.Pool'd buffers, runs a grid-indexed haversine spatial join and two group-bys concurrently, then five analyses in parallel goroutines, and prints a formatted terminal briefing with ASCII bars and MemStats telemetry. Builder and Strategy patterns are real, but all analyses live in one 320-line Execute method, there are no tests, every file has a BOM and zero indentation (never gofmt'd), and the README and run.sh are corrupted by a PowerShell paste.

### Stakeholder value
The five questions are well chosen and cross-dataset in form (ratios, vacancy cohorts, spatial proximity, district composite, channel equity) with named stakeholders. Their answers are worthless in practice because both inputs are generated by the student's own seeded generator with the effects planted in; e.g. Canton's '55x dumping multiplier' and 4,906 'hazards within 150 m' are artifacts of uniform request allocation and centroid jitter.
