Interpretation and limitations

Use the findings to guide neighborhood reviews and investigate differences in service-request categories. These comparisons do not establish causes or adjust for neighborhood size and request mix.

The 100-notice threshold is a project choice. Notice records are not necessarily unique buildings. Dataset time periods may differ. Recorded closure does not prove an issue was resolved.
