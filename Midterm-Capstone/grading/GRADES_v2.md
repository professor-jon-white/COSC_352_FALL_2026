4. **Bigelow, Nickolas** (88) — Mix-adjusted 311 wait index by agency across crime tiers (widest gap: Forestry/Rec & Parks), and day-of-week x hour patrol timing near liquor outlets.
5. **Rasheed, Wariz** (99) — Volume-adjusted search for which 311 types track violent crime, and night-time (21:00-03:59) violent crime by license-density tier.
6. **Dada, Daniel** (85) — Under-reporting z-score (crimes per 311 request) that flagged Inner Harbor at z=7.2, and neighborhoods whose monthly crime curve diverges from the city's (Brooklyn r=-0.27).
7. **Allen, Jordan** (88) — Lift analysis of 311 types in top-crime neighborhoods (SW-Dirty Alley Proactive 6.2x) and crime-vs-311 hour-of-day divergence for staffing.
8. **Morton, Tayven** (86) — Same-request-type closure comparison across vacancy cohorts, isolating parking complaints at +12.4 days in high-vacancy areas.
9. **Curbeam, Michael** (84) — Category-level correlation (sanitation r=+0.70 vs transportation +0.26) and ZIP-level code-enforcement targeting (21217, 21223, 21213).
10. **Jones, Xavier** (76) — Crime-share vs arrest-share gap by neighborhood (Downtown 3.6% of crime, 8.3% of arrests) as an enforcement-distribution signal.
11. **Arulogun, Obaloluwa** (52) — Intake-channel 'digital divide' by vacancy tier (phone vs app vs web). Novel idea, but answered on synthetic data so no real finding.
12. **Thomas, Tyriqe** (52) — Leave-one-out predictive test of district gun incidents. Novel method, but on a hand-typed 9-row table.
